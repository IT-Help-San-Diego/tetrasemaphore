# Tetrasemaphore as a deeper calculator

Tetrasemaphore does not ask what you believe. It asks where the claim is standing.

Humans already use tools when direct intuition is too weak for the job: an abacus or calculator for arithmetic, a compass for direction, a microscope for small reality, a compiler or checksum for code and data. Tetrasemaphore is the same kind of tool for thought: a deeper calculator for stance, frame, contradiction, and self-audit.

It does not replace belief, story, culture, or tradition. It provides a momentary instrument for calculation.

The four positions are mathematical stance segments that exist whether anyone believes in them or not:

| State | Symbol | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Normative | `I` | Is the claim standing inside the accepted frame? |
| Non-normative | `σᵥ` | Is the claim different from the frame without necessarily attacking it? |
| Critical | `C₂` | Is the claim testing, inverting, or challenging the frame? |
| Metacognitive | `σₕ` | Is the claim examining the frame itself? |

The system is “anti-myth” only in the sense that a calculator is anti-bad-arithmetic. It does not ban stories. It prevents a story from pretending to be the measurement.

Its purpose is to make unearned narrative harder to formulate and raw truth easier to read.

No magic. No prophecy. No new religion. Just math and logic, rendered as four sign-bearers.
