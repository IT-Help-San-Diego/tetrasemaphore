# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Films on the home page (`site/index.html`, `site/Makefile`, `site/verify.sh`, `site/infra/cloudfront/`):** Under each semaphore's still, a closed line opens that semaphore's film, and "How the four fit together" has the loop of all four. The stills are unchanged and remain the semaphores; before a film plays it shows the release's own still. Nothing is fetched until a reader presses play, and there is no script and no outside video host. The films served are the files in `semaphores/films/`, byte for byte, and the site's gate checks their hashes. The page's style block changed, so its hash changed in the page's policy and in the two CloudFront header policies.

- **Home page, three fixes from a PageSpeed report (`site/index.html`, `site/note.html`, `site/method.html`, `site/404.html`, `site/infra/cloudfront/`):** The in-page policy of each of the four pages no longer carries `frame-ancestors`, which a browser ignores, and reports as an error, when it arrives in a `<meta>` element; the two CloudFront header policies keep it, and the header is the copy browsers apply. On the home page the link around each of the four stills takes the width of its card, so the square a still will fill is held before it arrives and the grid no longer shifts when the four load. The four films take the 512 px still already on the page as their poster in place of the 1298 px picture, so a visit downloads nothing beyond the page's own stills until a reader presses play, about 2.9 MB less; the loop keeps the social card as its poster. The home page's style block changed, so its hash changed in the page's policy; the two CloudFront header policies carry the new hash beside the old one, so that the pages and the policies can go live in either order without a moment in which the home page's style sheet is refused, and the old one can be dropped once the page is live.

## [1.3.0] — 2026-11-23

Version DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24481385` (concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23575814`, which always resolves to the latest version). Continues 1.2.0 (`10.5281/zenodo.24448318`), 1.1.0 (`10.5281/zenodo.24267509`) and 1.0.0 (`10.5281/zenodo.23575816`), which stay published and citable. This release adds the films and a check of what the proofs' statements say, and corrects one sentence on the home page. The proof file, the masters, the pictures, the datasheets, the note and the PDFs are the files of 1.2.0, byte for byte.

- **Films (`semaphores/films/`, `tools/make_animation.py`):** Ten videos: one film for each semaphore under the three moves in turn, and one loop of all four, each as MP4 and as WebM. Every frame is made from the release's finished pictures, and the frame that ends each move is the picture with its rows, its columns, or both reversed, pixel for pixel; `python3 tools/make_animation.py --check-frames` tests that. The script makes the films. The published files are identified by their hashes in the manifest and not by a rebuild, because a video encoder's bytes differ from one build of it to the next.
- **Statement check (`proofs/comparator/`):** `proofs/check.sh` checks how the proofs were made, and says that it cannot tell whether a theorem says what its name says. This folder adds Lean's own judge for that, `lake comparator`. Claude Code wrote seventeen headline statements from the English of the note, the guide and the datasheets, with no proofs, and Hermes ran the judge on a Linux machine on 23 November 2026. Fifteen are, to the judge, the published statements, and their proofs pass Lean's kernel and five independent checkers. Two differ in form and not in meaning, and each equivalence is proved. Nothing false was found in the proofs. This is the project's own lanes checking one another and not an outside review; the folder's README says what the check does not show.
- **Correction, home page (`site/index.html`):** The page said that of the sixteen sets of moves "exactly five are closed". Six are: the empty set is closed too. The five meant are the ones that contain I and are closed, which is what the cited theorem `T8_five_are` proves, and the sentence now says so. Found on 23 November 2026 by a check of the proofs' statements against the English. The note and the PDFs never carried the sentence. The same page cited `Move.act_mul` alone for "Any two of the three moves make the third"; it now cites the table as well (`Move.mul, Move.act_mul`).
- **Found and left for a later version:** The check found three things that this release states and does not change. No single theorem says "any two of the three moves make the third", or "each move undoes itself", of the points of the plane; each is proved in two pieces. The datasheets say "the mark" where the theorems are about a model mark. The axiom counts in `proofs/README_LEAN.md` are those of Lean 4.33.0, the version the proofs pin; under Lean 4.36.0 four theorems also report `Classical.choice`. All three are set out in `proofs/comparator/README.md`.
- **Manifest, citation and specification (`RELEASE-MANIFEST.md`, `CITATION.cff`, `semaphores/README.md`, `docs/DATASHEET_SPEC.md`):** The manifest's guide names `semaphores/films/` and `proofs/comparator/`, and its rows are regenerated. The guide in `semaphores/` describes the films. The specification's status paragraph names this release.

## [1.2.0] — 2026-11-22

Version DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24448318` (concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23575814`, which always resolves to the latest version). Continues 1.1.0 (`10.5281/zenodo.24267509`) and 1.0.0 (`10.5281/zenodo.23575816`), which stay published and citable. A layout release: no picture, datasheet, PDF or page of the website changes by a byte.

- **A top folder for the finished files (`semaphores/`):** A reader looking for the semaphores had to go six folders down, into a folder named "proposals". `semaphores/vector-masters/` now holds the four layered SVG masters, `semaphores/pictures/` the 40 finished pictures (512, 1024, 1214, 1298 and 2460 px, in English and Spanish) and `semaphores/datasheets/` the four datasheets with their 1200 px previews, which move there from `site/assets/`. `semaphores/README.md` gives sizes, layers, colour, licence and how to cite. These are the files to use and to cite.
- **Build record (`build-record/`, named `artwork/` until now):** The folder is renamed for what it holds and gains a short README. Nothing inside it changed. It keeps the same masters and pictures, with its paths as they were built, as the evidence of how they were made. `tools/check_semaphores.py` is new: it stops unless each master and picture in `semaphores/` is byte for byte the build record's file and carries the hash that record's `MANIFEST.json` gives.
- **Datasheets (`semaphores/datasheets/`, `tools/build_datasheets.py`, `site/verify.sh`):** The eight pictures are the files published in 1.1.0 and cite that release. `build_datasheets.py --verify` finds them in their new folder, still stages its inputs from the tag the sheets cite, and reads the masters from `build-record/` where that tag is not available from git. The site's deploy gate no longer admits datasheet images under `site/assets/`.
- **PDFs (`pdf/`, `tools/generate_pdfs.py`):** Neither source changed, so both PDFs are the files earlier releases published, byte for byte: the public note is the 1.1.0 file and the methods note the 1.0.0 file, and each prints that release's version and DOI. The generator reads the pictures from `semaphores/pictures/` and prints in each colophon the folder they were in when that PDF was typeset.
- **Manifest and specification (`RELEASE-MANIFEST.md`, `docs/DATASHEET_SPEC.md`):** The manifest's guide names `semaphores/` and `build-record/`, and its rows are regenerated. The specification's status paragraph says where the datasheets now are.

## [1.1.0] — 2026-11-17

Version DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24267509` (concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23575814`, which always resolves to the latest version). Continues 1.0.0 (`10.5281/zenodo.23575816`), which stays published and citable.

- **Three text lines (`README.md`, `site/index.html`, `site/note.html`):** Approved by Carey James Balboa on 6 November 2026 after an audit of how the deployed text routes a reader. Across the three deployed pages, 29 sentences put a body in motion and none claimed a benefit from the movement, so nothing was faddish; but the routes offered were body 29, paper and print 27, mind 3, and the only sentence naming the mind put it second. The body was not pushy, it was standing alone, which made it look required. The checks introduction now names running a check in your head first and says why the physical routes exist: imagination is easy to fool, and turning a real card over settles what picturing it only suggests. The sentence on how the four moves are learned no longer prescribes an order. The METACOGNITIVE sentence no longer ends "this is thinking about thinking", whose "this" could be read as making the posture itself the cognitive act; the meaning is now attached to the move, which is what the algebra defines. The checks introduction and the METACOGNITIVE sentence are in all three files; the sentence on how the four moves are learned is on the home page only, as in 1.0.0. `README.md` and `site/note.html` also lose the em dash before the signature under Carey's statement in "Why we left the owl".
- **Datasheet toolchain (`tools/`, `docs/DATASHEET_SPEC.md`):** `build_datasheets.py`, `make_anatomy_plate.py`, `make_audit_report.py`, `measure_drafting.py`, `choose_drafting_colour.py`, `test_quotation_punctuation.py`, `drafting_measurements.json` and the two tables `handoff_art_colours.json` and `handoff_drafting_candidates.json`, with the pinned build environment in `tools/datasheets-environment/`. The driver stages its inputs from the release the sheets cite (`git show <tag>:<path>`), so a sheet provably quotes the note it names, and `--verify` proves a clean checkout regenerates the committed sheets byte for byte.
- **Four datasheets, as release files (`site/assets/datasheet_*.png`, `docs/datasheet-audit/`, `docs/AUDIT_REPORT.md`):** One sheet per semaphore, 2400 × 1500 with a 1200 px preview, built to `docs/DATASHEET_SPEC.md`. Every cited string is looked up in the published note before anything is drawn and every Lean name in the published proof file: 116 strings provenance-checked with no faults, 29 quotation spans with no punctuation faults, no text overlaps and nothing outside the canvas. A sheet now identifies itself as a release file only when it carries a release DOI, and prints a mock-up line otherwise, so the claim cannot be made before it is true. The build stops if a sheet, an audit record or the audit report carries an em dash in its own words.
- **Standfirst elision (`tools/make_anatomy_plate.py`):** The METACOGNITIVE sheet quotes the first sentence of its note entry and marks the elision, as `DATASHEET_SPEC.md` §3 requires. The gate is unchanged: the **unelided** sentence is what is looked up in the note, and the build still stops if it is absent; only what is drawn is short. The audit JSON records both, under `text` and `unelided`.
- **Public note PDF (`pdf/TETRASEMAPHORE-PUBLIC-NOTE.pdf`, `tools/generate_pdfs.py`):** Typeset again from this version's `README.md`, so it carries the lines above, this version's number and this version's DOI. The methods note PDF is the file published in 1.0.0, byte for byte, because its source did not change; it prints that release's version and DOI. The generator records which release each PDF was typeset for and stops if a build differs from that release's file.
- **Website between the two releases (`site/`):** Changes made after 1.0.0 and recorded here for the first time: the social card image; the section that takes the four semaphores apart; the "How this was made" section cut to one sentence; Zenodo and ORCID links added to the footer; em dashes removed and claims sourced; the 56rem column restored and a flashcard line added to each semaphore's section; the deploy gate changed to admit the datasheet images, which the deploy list leaves out, to check a style hash for each page, and to let `tools/handoff_art_colours.json` and `tools/handoff_drafting_candidates.json` past its check of file names; the two response-header policies carry the pages' style hashes. In this version the Zenodo link on every page takes the concept DOI, `10.5281/zenodo.23575814`, which resolves to the latest version, and `sitemap.xml` gives this version's release day for the three pages.
- **Manifest (`RELEASE-MANIFEST.md`, `tools/make_release_manifest.py`):** Rebuilt by a script from the files git tracks: a row for every file except itself, `CHANGELOG.md` and `CITATION.cff`, and a check that fails on a file without a row. Its guide now names `docs/` and `tools/`, and its register of what is left out is brought up to date.
- **Fonts and licences (`tools/fonts/`, `NOTICE`):** The three DejaVu font files the datasheets are set in are in the release with their own licence text. They are other people's work under their own licence, and `NOTICE` and the manifest now say so. `NOTICE` also says that the DejaVu fonts embedded in the two PDFs stay under that licence, and that words quoted from others and the texts of the licences are not relicensed by this project.
- **Datasheet specification (`docs/DATASHEET_SPEC.md`):** Its status paragraph describes release files and no longer a proposal, and the build stops if a cited line range does not contain the string it cites.
